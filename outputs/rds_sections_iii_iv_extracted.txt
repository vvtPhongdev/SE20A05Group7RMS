III. Design Specifications

This section translates the RMS requirement specifications into implementable screen, service, database and integration designs. UI labels may be refined during usability review, but access scope, workflow transitions and data ownership are mandatory.

1. System Architecture and Shared Platform

1.1 High-Level Runtime Design

a. Screen / Function Description

RMS is a TypeScript monorepo. React/Vite communicates with the NestJS HTTP Gateway. The Gateway validates JWT and roles, then calls Identity, Recruiting, Profiles/CV, Interview and Notification services. PostgreSQL is shared through Prisma; Redis/BullMQ handles asynchronous CV parsing, embeddings and reminders.

Related use cases: UC-60, UC-61 and all cross-cutting use cases

Identity, Recruiting, Profiles/CV, Interview and Notification business logic.

6. Interview Scheduling, Calendar and Evaluation

2. Limitations & Exclusions

ID

Limitation / Exclusion

LE-01

RMS supports recruitment workflow management; it is not a payroll, attendance or employee-performance system.

LE-02

AI assists CV extraction and search; it does not make autonomous hiring decisions.

LE-03

Semantic ranking quality depends on parsed CV content and successful embedding generation. Newly uploaded CVs may not be searchable until asynchronous jobs finish.

LE-04

Google Calendar reminders do not guarantee email delivery; strict delivery requires the RMS notification/email worker.

LE-05

Calendar conflict detection is limited to accessible calendars and information granted by users.

LE-06

Offline interview evidence is retained for review but is not a digital-signature or legal-verification service.

LE-07

The MVP uses a shared PostgreSQL database; independent database-per-service deployment is excluded.

LE-08

Annual export supports implemented CSV/PDF formats only; arbitrary report-builder functionality is excluded.

LE-09

Mobile native applications are excluded; responsive web access is the supported client.

LE-10

External job boards, HRIS, payroll and background-check integrations are excluded unless separately configured.

LE-11

The system does not guarantee extraction accuracy for encrypted, scanned or malformed CV documents.

LE-12

Disaster recovery, multi-region active-active deployment and unlimited retention are operational extensions beyond the MVP.



3. Business Rules

ID

Category

Rule Definition

BR-01

Security

Every protected API requires a valid access token and an authorized role.

BR-02

Identity

Inactive users cannot authenticate or perform protected business actions.

BR-03

Scope

Users may access only organization, department, request, candidate or offer records permitted by their role and ownership.

BR-04

Request workflow

A recruitment request must follow valid state transitions; rejection, revision and request-change actions require a reason.

BR-05

Approval

Only Admin performs final request approval and final hiring decision.

BR-06

Plan lock

Sourcing, screening, interview and offer activities are blocked until the related OverallPlan is approved/active.

BR-07

Task planning

Task dates must be within the OverallPlan period; only eligible active HR users may be assigned.

BR-08

CV processing

A CV becomes semantically searchable only after parse success and creation of a 384-dimensional embedding.

BR-09

Search

Hybrid ranking may combine vector similarity, skill-graph proximity and requirement coverage; ranking is advisory.

BR-10

Interview panel

A scheduled interview requires at least two distinct active internal panel members.

BR-11

Interview mode

ONLINE requires a meeting link or successfully generated Meet link; OFFLINE requires a venue and may require supporting evidence for Admin review.

BR-12

Calendar

Meet creation uses authorized Google Calendar credentials; attendee updates are sent to configured participants.

BR-13

Feedback

Only authorized panel/HR evaluators may submit feedback; an evaluator cannot impersonate another evaluator.

BR-14

Decision

HIRE requires candidateId, compensation and startDate; REJECT or REQUEST_INFO requires an explanatory reason/note.

BR-15

Offer ownership

Only the candidate who owns an offer may accept or decline it; a finalized response cannot be silently overwritten.

BR-16

Notifications

Notifications are user-scoped; only the owner may read, mark, delete or stream them.

BR-17

Audit

Material workflow, role, decision and status changes must write RequestLog and/or AuditLog records.

BR-18

Reminders

Deadline reminders must be idempotent and must not create duplicate notification/email logs for the same reminder point.

BR-19

Reporting

Department Heads see their department only; HR and Admin report scope follows configured role permissions.

BR-20

Data integrity

Database mutations that create related decision, offer, log and notification records must be transactional where consistency requires it.
