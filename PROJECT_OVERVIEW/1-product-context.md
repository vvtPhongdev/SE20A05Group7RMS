# 1. Product Context

## What Is This?

Works Recruiter (RMS) is an **enterprise hiring platform** connecting **Department Heads**, **Hiring Managers**, **HR Recruiters**, and **Candidates** through AI-assisted capability matching. Instead of opaque "Match 87%" scores, the system produces **readiness labels** backed by traceable evidence from CVs, job descriptions, and a capability knowledge graph.

## Who Does It Serve?

| Persona | Role | Primary Goal |
|---------|------|-------------|
| **Department Head** | Creates hiring requests, writes JD drafts, sees candidate cards (read-only, no contact/salary info) | Initiate and monitor department-level hiring needs |
| **Hiring Manager** | Approves/rejects hiring requests through multi-level approval chain (max 3 levels, configurable) | Ensure hiring decisions meet organizational standards |
| **HR Recruiter** | Executes recruitment — publishes jobs, discovers candidates, reviews evidence, manages pipeline, exports hiring packets | Defend hiring recommendations with transparent reasoning |
| **Candidate** | Builds profile, discovers matching jobs, applies with smart proposals, tracks applications | Participate as co-author of their professional narrative |
| **Admin** | Configures organizations, departments, and approval chains | Platform administration and configuration |

## Core Problem

Traditional ATS platforms match keywords. A CV listing "React, Node.js, PostgreSQL" may never say "web developer" — yet that candidate is highly relevant for a fullstack role. Senior engineering roles are worse: no keyword can capture architecture judgment, production readiness, or ownership mindset.

**Result:** Expensive marketplace noise on both sides — recruiters can't defend recommendations, candidates waste effort on black-hole applications.
